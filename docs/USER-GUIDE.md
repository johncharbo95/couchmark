# Using Couchmark

Everything is done with the Roku remote. If you haven't installed Couchmark yet, start with [Installing](INSTALL.md) and [Setting up your USB drive](USB-SETUP.md).

## The remote at a glance

| Button | What it does in Couchmark |
|---|---|
| **Arrows** | Move around. Left/Right also skip 10 seconds while a video plays. |
| **OK** | Open or play what's highlighted. Pauses a video. |
| **Play/Pause** | Starts a movie or episode straight away, without opening its page. Pauses / resumes a video. |
| **✱** (star / options) | Mark as watched / unwatched. Shows photo details in a slideshow. |
| **◀◀ / ▶▶** | Click to skip 30 seconds, hold to scrub. Previous / next photo in a slideshow. |
| **↺** (instant replay) | Back 10 seconds. |
| **Back** | Go back one screen. Stops a video and saves your place. On the home screen, it closes Couchmark. |

Your place is saved automatically whenever you stop or leave a video, so you never need to remember where you were.

## Home

The home screen works like Netflix. Up/Down moves between rows, Left/Right scrolls a row, and the top of the screen describes the highlighted title.

The date and time are shown at the top right in 24-hour format. Your Roku sets its clock from the internet; the time zone comes from the Roku's **Settings → System → Time**.

- **Continue Watching:** everything you're partway through, most recent first. Press **OK once** to resume exactly where you stopped, or to start the next episode if you finished one.
- **Shows** and **Movies:** your whole library. A blue bar shows how far you got, and a ✓ means watched (past 90%).
- **Browse:** A–Z lists of all shows and movies, **Other** (slideshows), **Files** (every folder on the drive), and **Settings**.

Back on the home screen exits Couchmark.

## Play/Pause to start right away

Press **Play/Pause** (instead of OK) on a movie or episode to start it straight away, without opening its page:

| Where | What Play/Pause does |
|---|---|
| A **Continue Watching** tile | Resumes it (same as OK) |
| A movie (home row, A–Z list, or any button on the movie's page) | Resumes where you stopped, or plays from the beginning |
| An episode (show page) | Plays it, resuming where you stopped. No "Resume / Start over" question; press OK for that. |
| The **Continue** button (show page) | Same as OK |
| A video in **Files** | Plays it, resuming where you stopped (inside Shows/Movies) |

On a show tile, a season or the Browse row, Play/Pause does nothing.

## Movies

A movie's page offers **Resume** and **Play from beginning** (or just **Play**). If a movie folder holds several video files, they play one after another as parts. During the last 10 seconds of a part, an **Up next** card counts down: press **OK** to start the next part right away, or let it finish and the next part starts by itself.

## Shows

A show's page has a **Continue** button at the top, seasons on the left, and that season's episodes on the right:

- **Continue S02E05 at 23:14** if you stopped partway through an episode
- **Next: S02E06** if you finished the last one
- **Start watching S01E01** if you've never watched it

The row with a **white background** is the cursor: that's what OK acts on. There is only ever one. The season whose episodes are shown has a **blue bar** on its left and a blue tint, and keeps it while you browse its episodes.

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| **Left** (in the episodes) | Move to the season list. The cursor starts on the season you're watching. |
| **Up / Down** (in the seasons) | Move the cursor. The episodes don't change yet. |
| **OK** or **Right** (on a season) | Open that season: its episodes appear and the cursor jumps to them, on the episode you'd continue with, or else the first one you haven't watched |
| **Up** (on the top row) | Back to the Continue button |
| **Down** (on Continue) | To the episodes |

Episodes show ✓ when watched and a blue bar with the time left when partly watched. During the last 10 seconds of an episode, an **Up next** card appears in the corner and counts down. From that moment the episode counts as watched ✓, even if you stop during the credits. Press **OK** to start the next episode right away, or let it finish and the next one starts by itself, including into the next season. Pause, rewind and the other buttons keep working while the card is showing; rewinding hides it.

Episode files are ordered by their `S01E02` / `1x02` code when they have one, otherwise naturally by name. Video files placed directly in a show folder (no season folder) appear under "Episodes".

## Files (every folder on the drive)

If a show or movie doesn't appear the way you expect, open **Browse → Files**. It lists the top of every USB drive exactly as Couchmark sees it, with no sorting into shows and movies: folders first, then files, with a count at the top (for example *2 folders · 24 videos*).

- **OK on a folder** opens it.
- **OK on a video** plays it; the videos after it in the same folder play next. **Play/Pause** starts it straight away.
- **OK on a photo** starts a slideshow of that folder.
- Files Couchmark can't play are listed as **Can't play**, so you can see they're there.

Videos inside a show's folder (`Shows / <Show> / <Season>`) or a movie's folder (`Movies / <Movie>`) share their progress with the show or movie page: they resume where you stopped, show ✓ and progress bars, and count toward **Continue**. Videos anywhere else play without saving a resume point.

## Other (slideshows)

Open **Browse → Other** to see the folders inside `Other`. In any folder that contains photos or videos, the first row is **Play slideshow**. It shows how many photos and videos the folder holds, and how many files can't be shown (for example HEIC photos). The slideshow includes the files directly in that folder, not its subfolders.

- Items play **in the order they were taken**: the camera date for JPEG photos, the recording date for MP4/MOV videos, and otherwise the file's modified date.
- Photos stay on screen for 5 seconds and change with a fade (both adjustable in Settings). Videos play to the end. After the last item, the slideshow starts over.
- Portrait phone photos are rotated upright automatically.

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| **OK** or **Play/Pause** | Pause / resume |
| **Left / ◀◀** | Previous photo or video |
| **Right / ▶▶** | Next photo or video |
| **Up / Down / \*** | Show the date, file name and position (e.g. 12 / 240) |
| **Back** | Leave the slideshow |

## Marking as watched / unwatched

Press **✱** (the star/options button) on:

| Where | What ✱ does |
|---|---|
| A movie (home row, A–Z list, or the movie's page) | Watched ✓ ↔ unwatched. Marking watched clears its resume point. The movie page also has a **Mark as watched** button. |
| A **Continue Watching** tile | "I've already seen this": marks that movie or episode watched, and the row moves on to the next episode. |
| An episode (show page) | Watched ✓ ↔ unwatched |
| A season (show page, left list, under the cursor) | Marks every episode in it watched. If they all are already, marks them all unwatched. |
| The **Continue** button (show page) | Marks that episode watched, so Continue moves on |

A short message confirms each change. Marking episodes watched moves the show's **Continue** forward, never backwards. Marking an earlier season doesn't lose your place in a later one.

## Player controls

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| **OK** or **Play/Pause** | Pause / resume (shows the controls) |
| **Left / Right** | Back / forward 10 seconds |
| **↺** (instant replay) | Back 10 seconds |
| **◀◀ / ▶▶** (click) | Back / forward exactly 30 seconds. Quick clicks add up: three clicks = 90 seconds. |
| **◀◀ / ▶▶** (hold) | Scrub: the picture pauses and the progress bar moves, faster the longer you hold (30x, then 60x, then 120x). Let go to continue from there. |
| **Down** | Move to the button row: Previous episode · Back 10 · Play/Pause · Forward 10 · Next episode. Use Left/Right to choose and OK to press. |
| **Up** | Back to the progress bar |
| **Back** | Stop and return. Your position is saved. |

Under the progress bar, the left side shows the time played and the right side shows the time left and the total length (e.g. `-23:14 / 44:00`). The controls hide after 3 seconds, or stay visible while paused. For movies with several parts, Previous/Next move between parts. Single-file movies have no Previous/Next buttons.

Skipping back or forward (Left/Right, ↺, ◀◀/▶▶ clicks, Back 10 / Forward 10) keeps the video as it was: if it was paused, it stays paused at the new spot; if it was playing, it keeps playing.

If no drive is found, plug one in. Couchmark notices it automatically, or you can press **OK** to look again.

## Settings

Open **Browse → Settings**. Use Up/Down to choose a setting and Left/Right (or OK) to change it. Changes are saved immediately.

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| Photo duration | 3, 5, 8, 10, 15 or 30 seconds |
| Transition | None, Fade, Slide, Push, Wipe, Zoom, Random |
| Transition speed | Slow (1.5 s), Normal (0.8 s), Fast (0.4 s) |
| Save debug logs to USB | On (default) or Off. See [Debug logs](#debug-logs). The help line shows where the log is being saved. |
| Clear watch history | Press OK twice. Forgets all resume points and ✓ marks; your files are not touched. |

## Debug logs

If something goes wrong (an episode is missing, a video won't play, the app closes), the log shows what Couchmark saw. Unplug the drive, put it in a computer, and open:

```
Couchmark/logs/couchmark-2026-10-24.log      ← one file per day
```

Any text editor can open it. Each line has the time, a level (`INFO`, `WARN`, `ERROR`), where it came from, and what happened:

```
2026-10-24 21:05:03.042 INFO  [App] Couchmark 0.2.0 starting
2026-10-24 21:05:04.310 INFO  [UsbScanTask] volumes=["ext1:"] roots={"movies":["ext1:/Movies"],...}
2026-10-24 21:06:12.800 INFO  [ShowScanTask] ext1:/Shows/The Office/Season 1: 14 listed, 12 videos; skipped: S01E07.avi.part, notes.nfo
2026-10-24 21:07:40.115 ERROR [Player] playback error: ext1:/Movies/X/X.mkv | errorMsg=... | errorCode=-5
```

Useful things to look for:

- **Missing episode or movie:** find the `ShowScanTask` or `FolderScanTask` line for its folder. It counts the files the TV listed and the videos kept, and names the files that were skipped (unsupported extension, hidden file, …).
- **A video won't play:** search for `ERROR`. The `Player` line has the file and the Roku's error message and code.
- **Drive not found:** `UsbScanTask` lists every volume the TV reports and the folders at the top of each drive. `USB` lines show drives being plugged in or removed.

Details:

- The log goes on the first USB drive that can be written. Couchmark keeps the last 7 days and each file stays under about 256 KB, so it never fills the drive. Your media files are never touched.
- If no drive can be written (for example a file system the Roku can only read, or no drive plugged in), the log is kept on the TV in temporary storage instead and is lost when the TV restarts. Lines written there are copied to the drive once it's available again.
- Lines are saved about once a second (errors immediately), so the last second before a crash may be missing.
- Nothing is ever sent off your TV. Turn it off with **Settings → Save debug logs to USB**.
